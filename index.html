The Space

More Than Play.
This Is Where Kids Actually Grow.

Little Bee is an indoor play space for kids ages 0–6 in Lexington, SC. We keep sessions small on purpose — so your child gets to actually play, not wait in line or compete for space.

🧠

Built Around the Senses

Touch, sound, movement, light — every corner of this space has something to discover. We built it around how kids actually experience the world, not how adults think they should.

🛡️

Safe by Design

We cap every session so it never gets chaotic. Grip socks required, waivers on file, staff always on the floor. You can actually relax while they play.

🌈

Inclusive & Welcoming

Kids with sensory sensitivities belong here. We keep sessions calm and low-key on purpose — so every child can show up, feel comfortable, and just play.

☕

The Buzz Café

While the kids do their thing, grab a snack or a drink at The Buzz. You've earned a minute to sit down.

Good to Know

Before You Visit

📋

Waiver Required

Everyone needs a signed waiver before entering the play area. Fill it out online before you come so you're not doing paperwork at the door.

🧦

Socks Required

Grip socks are required for all children. Forget yours? We sell them at the front for $3.

📅

Book Ahead

Sessions must be booked online. We don't hold spots for walk-ins, and we cap capacity on purpose to keep it from getting loud and crowded.

⏰

Arrive on Time

Your session ends at the same time regardless of when you walk in. Give yourself a few extra minutes when you arrive.

🍽️

No Outside Food

No outside food or drinks during open play. Snacks and drinks are available at The Buzz Café inside.

👨‍👩‍👧

Adults Stay With Kids

One adult per child is included. Extra adults are $3 each. All adults are expected to stay and watch their children — this is not a drop-off facility.

🐝

Ready to Book?

Spots fill up fast, especially on weekends. Book now and give your little one something to look forward to.
